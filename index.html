About Us

Since its founding in 2013, Siam Flex Technology, the former company of SRM Key Solutions LLC, has established itself as a trusted supplier of industrial spare parts and engineering solutions to contractors and customers across a wide range of heavy industries, including power generation, steel mills, refineries, oil & gas, food processing, mining and minerals, and other industrial sectors.

With extensive experience in global sourcing and worldwide supply, we have built a strong international network of manufacturers and suppliers, enabling us to source and supply industrial parts and equipment from around the world. Our expertise allows us to respond efficiently to diverse customer requirements while ensuring the correct specifications, reliable quality, competitive solutions, and timely delivery.

Today, SRM Key Solutions LLC continues to expand its global capabilities, connecting trusted manufacturers worldwide with customers across international markets. Our commitment is to provide dependable sourcing and supply solutions that help our customers maintain reliable and efficient operations.
